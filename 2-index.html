THE SPIRAL CURSE OF KURŌZU-CHO

The fog came in off the lake and never left. Spirals bloom in the town now — in the pottery, in the shells, in the clouds, in the people. Seven cursed spirals anchor the obsession to Kurōzu-cho. Seal them all before the pattern notices you. Do not stare at the spirals. They stare back.

VR (Quest 3): Enter VR — left stick walks, right stick snap-turns, hold trigger on a spiral to seal it
Desktop: WASD walk, mouse look, hold click on a spiral to seal it
Looking at spirals feeds the curse. Snail-folk are no longer people. Avoid them.
